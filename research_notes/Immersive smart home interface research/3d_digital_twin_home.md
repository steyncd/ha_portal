# 3D, Spatial and "Digital Twin" Visualisations of a Real Home for Home Automation (state as of 18 November 2026)

Scope note: research was done by web search and page fetches on 18 Nov 2026. Where a fact came only from a search-result summary rather than a fetched page, it is marked "(search summary)". Target environment for every fit assessment: browser-only Svelte 5 PWA on iPhone/iPad Safari (installed), desktop Chrome/Edge, and a low-power TV / wall-display browser.

---

## 1. Notable Home Assistant community 3D / floor-plan dashboards of 2024-2026

### Takeaway
2026 saw an explosion of three.js-based Home Assistant "live 3D house" projects: the long-stale `floor3d-card` (three.js r130, 2021) was revived by two actively-developed forks on three.js r186, and at least five new "draw your house inside HA" integrations (Diorama, NeonPlan 3D, NextFloor, Homeslice, Hollako's 3D Floorplan) appeared between June and November 2026 — almost all single-maintainer, weeks-to-months old, and several openly AI-assisted. The most instructive example for a bespoke web front end is not a card at all but a hand-built Blender-to-glTF + vanilla three.js site driven by the HA WebSocket API, which publishes concrete numbers (7.8 MB Draco glTF, ~499k triangles, ~1,030 draw calls, pooled point lights, 512 px texture cap on touch) for running on an iPad in Safari.

### Cited Findings

#### A. The `floor3d-card` lineage (model-import cards, three.js)
- The original `floor3d-card` ("Your Home Digital Twin") is a Lovelace JS module that loads a 3D model and binds objects to entity states; it is the ancestor of all the forks below — [GitHub adizanni/floor3d-card](https://github.com/adizanni/floor3d-card), [community thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/your-home-digital-twin-interactive-floor-3d-plan/301549)
- Maintenance status of the original: a fork author says the original repo "hasn't been updated since 2024" and that it is pinned at three.js 0.130; a German review says its last release was 2023. Treat it as abandoned — [floor3d-card mod thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/floor3d-card-mod-updated-fork-of-the-3d-floor-plan-card-three-js-0-186-lit-3-sunlight-new-editor/1026920); [simon42 Diorama article](https://www.simon42.com/diorama-home-assistant-3d-karte/)
- Known original-card pain point: too many WebGL contexts cause the oldest to be lost, so users are told to display only one floor3d card at a time (search summary) — [community thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/your-home-digital-twin-interactive-floor-3d-plan/301549)
- **floor3d-card mod** (posted 30 Sep 2026; fork of v1.5.3; MIT; keeps the `custom:floor3d-card` type so existing configs work): three.js 0.130 -> 0.186, Lit 3, Rollup 4, TypeScript 5.9; sRGB colour management, physical light units, Neutral tone mapping with exposure; sunlight follows `sun.sun` with time-of-day shadows; **person trackers that accept coordinate-reporting presence radars such as the HLK-LD2450**; rooms shaded by temperature or presence; doors coloured by state; animated shower; TV screen that shows the media player's picture and tints the room light; tap and long-press actions; editor rebuilt on HA's own components; nothing is rendered while the card is hidden. Tested only on HA 2026.9 — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/floor3d-card-mod-updated-fork-of-the-3d-floor-plan-card-three-js-0-186-lit-3-sunlight-new-editor/1026920)
- floor3d-card mod developer notes (highest version on page: 2.13.4): shadow-casting lights are capped by GPU texture units (16-unit GPU -> 14, 32-unit -> 30); `quality: low` = pixel ratio 1 on a 2x screen, shadows from the sun plus 4 lamps, 1024 px sun shadow map; `quality: auto` picks low at 2 GB device memory and high at 8 GB; animation loop stops when idle; doors/covers open around a hinge or slide and use `current_position`; sensor maps colour rooms by value with a legend; an alarm makes its room blink; a heater glows while heating — [FORK.md](https://github.com/giosci1994/floor3d-card/blob/master/FORK.md)
- **Floor3D Pro Card** ("Game Engine Backbone Edition", thread opened 16 Jan 2026; version 1.5.3-Pro.Faz.x): entity bindings of type light, hide, show, color, text, room, door, cover, rotate, gesture, camera; multi-level navigation by layer naming (`lvl001...`); render scheduler coalesces "100 triggers" into 1 render; a `mobile` skill sets pixel ratio 1, BasicShadowMap, no antialiasing; OBJ+MTL or GLB (GLB recommended). User reports: lag with more than 6 lights. Author active through Feb 2026; GitHub account was temporarily suspended then restored — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/floor3d-pro-card-game-engine-backbone-edition/975682)
- **Floor3DX Card** (posted 22 Oct 2026; MIT; fork of floor3dpro-card; versions 2.4 -> 2.18 within ~10 days): three.js r186, Lit 3, TS 5; adds **Draco, Meshopt, KTX2 and WebP** model compression with decoders shipped alongside the card; lights driven by entity brightness and colour; pictures and video on any surface; daylight system; themes; transparent walls. Installs under its own element name next to the older cards — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/floor3dx-card-the-3d-floor-plan-card-plus-two-optional-companions-a-2d-snapshot-card-and-a-learning-enabled-scheduler/1029922)
- Floor3DX published performance numbers: a 30 MB house model compressed to about 4 MB and loads "in a couple of seconds on a phone"; merging static meshes cut draw calls from 3,054 to 401 and frame CPU time from 26 ms to 8 ms with a pixel-identical image; shader compilation dominated start-up on Windows until setup was staged; a `light_rooms` option confines each lamp to its room polygon without shadow maps ("suits phones") at the cost of no light spill through doorways — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/floor3dx-card-the-3d-floor-plan-card-plus-two-optional-companions-a-2d-snapshot-card-and-a-learning-enabled-scheduler/1029922)
- Floor3DX's companion **Snapshot Card** renders the 3D model once in the browser to a static tappable 2D floor plan, auto-detects rooms and places entities, explicitly because "wall tablets and phones don't run 3D": "Tablets then load a 34 KB module and two images; no 3D runs on them." Has a guest mode that limits locks, garage doors and alarms. Author states the code was written with AI assistance — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/floor3dx-card-the-3d-floor-plan-card-plus-two-optional-companions-a-2d-snapshot-card-and-a-learning-enabled-scheduler/1029922)
- **Floorplan 3D** (johnnyo21): three.js card rendering Sweet Home 3D `.obj/.mtl` exports; author reports it bogs down on a phone with a large model; last pushed June 2025, likely less maintained (search summary) — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/floorplan-3d-a-home-assistant-3d-floorplan-card/902878)

#### B. Model-import cards with their own lighting model
- **Home Assistant 3D Floorplan** (Hollako; thread 3 Jun 2026, replies to 17 Sep 2026; README mentions v2.12.3; 42 stars): loads a single `.glb` from `/config/www/`, three.js 0.165.0 (default from esm.sh, can be bundled for offline); entity markers placed by clicking the model in Edit Mode; four light render types — **Spot** (ceiling downlight cone + floor hotspot + GI bounce), **Cove** (indirect ceiling wash), **Linear** (LED strip drawn as a path), **Lamp** (tight pool with soft wall glow) — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/ready-made-3d-floorplan-for-home-assistant-physics-based-lighting-fully-offline-no-extra-tools/1012715); [README](https://github.com/Hollako/Home-Assistant-3D-Floorplan)
- Its key technique: floor pools, ceiling glows and GI-bounce meshes are **clipped to zone polygons** so "light cannot bleed through walls into adjacent rooms regardless of pool radius"; zones have "Area" (flat ambient fill) or "Positional" (per-light pools) modes; day/night shade is driven by an illuminance sensor (300 lux = day shade) with `sun.sun` as fallback; GI bounce is off by default for performance — [README](https://github.com/Hollako/Home-Assistant-3D-Floorplan)
- Reported limitations: layouts save to the browser (users ask why they do not sync across devices); on-screen nav buttons take too much space on mobile; iOS/Android companion apps can block remote module imports so offline bundling is recommended; one user reports it "runs extremly smooth" on an old Surface wall tablet — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/ready-made-3d-floorplan-for-home-assistant-physics-based-lighting-fully-offline-no-extra-tools/1012715); [README](https://github.com/Hollako/Home-Assistant-3D-Floorplan)
- **3DHome** (4 stars, 5 commits, no releases — tiny/early): a Sweet Home 3D exporter plugin that writes GLB or split glTF with HA-oriented naming (`light_<name>`) and metadata in glTF extras, plus a card with a top-down orthographic three.js view. Lights are **not** three.js lights: state is shown with **emissive materials** on exported geometry; rendering pauses unless state, camera or config changes. README says bloom, shadows or animated glow "would call for a different approach" — [GitHub lukevink/3DHome](https://github.com/lukevink/3DHome)
- **3Dash**: a standalone web app (not a card) that loads your own 3D model and maps it to entities, built with React, TypeScript, **Babylon.js**, Vite and the HA WebSocket API, shipped with an HA add-on (search summary) — [GitHub Kdcius/3Dash_webapp](https://github.com/Kdcius/3Dash_webapp)
- **Zircon3D v3.0** (post 28 Sep 2026): 3D floorplan with floating control cards, floor- and room-level heatmaps (temperature, humidity, air quality), a "Room Box View" for occupancy/alarms, and visualisation of solar panels, batteries, EV chargers and electrical routing; integrates through a proxy add-on. Renderer, pricing and editor not stated in the post; only reply says install instructions are outdated — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/zircon3d-v3-0-3d-iot-visualization-for-smart-homes-small-businesses-and-smart-buildings/1026672)

#### C. The late-2026 "draw your house inside Home Assistant" wave (no external modelling tool)
- **Diorama** (`ha-diorama`; repo online since mid-Sep 2026, v1.8.29 at 29 Oct 2026, in the HACS default list since 21 Oct; MIT): full-screen built-in editor — draw rooms on a grid, place furniture and lamps that snap to walls, link each piece to an entity. Clicking a floor lamp in the model toggles the real lamp; lamps light their room and cast shadows; the sun moves with time of day; optional sidebar of per-device widgets grouped by room. Reviewer's verdict: a normal dashboard is faster for daily phone use, but for a hallway wall tablet or showing guests it is "pretty much exactly what you want" — [simon42](https://www.simon42.com/diorama-home-assistant-3d-karte/)
- **NeonPlan 3D** (post 2 Oct 2026, replies to 10 Nov; versions cited 1.15.0 and 2.0.5; MIT core with paid furniture packs / Pro add-ons; online demo at neonplan3d.mastershort.de): built-in editor for floors, rooms, walls, doors, windows, stairs, furniture, roof; rooms link to HA areas so lights/covers/sensors are found automatically; three looks (Neon, Blueprint, Day); sunlight from `sun.sun`; **tap switches, swipe dims, long-press opens colours**; temperature/humidity/CO2 heatmaps; cameras turn red while detecting motion; parking spots show the car when home; kiosk mode with idle return and night dimming; a "Tablet" quality level for old Fire tablets — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/neonplan-3d-draw-your-home-in-home-assistant-and-control-it-in-a-live-3d-floor-plan-free-hacs-online-demo/1027224)
- **NextFloor** (fork of NeonPlan 3D; started 8 Oct 2026; v0.16.1 with 15 releases in ~4 weeks; ~60 stars): "free forever" variant bundling 26 furniture packs (620+ models), Sweet Home 3D plan import, energy-flow visualisation, weather (rain), a **time-travel playback of recent activity**, and alarms for smoke/gas/water/open windows. Reviewer had not tested it and flags it as pre-1.0, single-developer, AI-assisted — [simon42](https://www.simon42.com/nextfloor-home-assistant-3d-plan/)
- **Homeslice** (post 3 Nov 2026; "early alpha"; MIT; live demo at headtr1ck.github.io/homeslice): house is built from HA floors (levels) and areas (rooms); you trace rooms over a floor-plan image **or a robot-vacuum map** and walls are generated. Views: exploded 3D, tilted plan, flat 2D, all floors side by side, 2D side view, section cuts. Styles: day, night, white model, blueprint. People and pets appear in their rooms; temperature/humidity/CO2 heatmap; animated solar/battery/grid flows; real sun position and weather; 400+ built-in furniture pieces that react to state (screens show playing content, fans turn); house defined in short YAML intended to be edited by AI assistants; redraws only on change; low-quality and kiosk modes. Author says it was mostly built with Claude Code — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/homeslice-your-home-as-a-live-3d-floor-plan-with-the-editor-built-into-home-assistant/1032185)

#### D. Bespoke Blender + three.js site driven by HA (best documented reference build)
- "My house as a live 3D dashboard" (thread started 29 Sep 2026, updates to 27 Oct 2026; no public repo): the house is generated **procedurally in headless Blender 5.2 LTS via a Python `bpy` script** that rebuilds the whole scene each run; exported as **glTF with Draco, 7.8 MB**; custom object properties (kind, room, `ha_entity`, hinge axis, angles) travel as **glTF extras** — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/my-house-as-a-live-3d-dashboard-blender-model-three-js-driven-by-home-assistant/1026746)
- Front end: **three.js 0.170 from a CDN via import map, no build step, no framework**; HA connection over WebSocket (`subscribe_events`, `weather/subscribe_forecast`); rooms map to HA areas so most entities are discovered automatically (91 entities bound in the model, 202 entity IDs referenced); every action goes through HA services — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/my-house-as-a-live-3d-dashboard-blender-model-three-js-driven-by-home-assistant/1026746)
- Lighting: 59 lights in the model (39 inside, 20 outside); a **fixed pool of point lights (14 on desktop, 6 on touch) is reassigned to the lamps nearest the camera so the shader never recompiles**; sun position computed from real location and orientation with a time slider; cloud shadows; night windows glow from HA light state; LED strips follow real paths. No baked lightmaps mentioned — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/my-house-as-a-live-3d-dashboard-blender-model-three-js-driven-by-home-assistant/1026746)
- Interaction: tap a lamp toggles it; **long-press opens a dimmer and colour card**; room cards show 24 h sparklines from HA history — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/my-house-as-a-live-3d-dashboard-blender-model-three-js-driven-by-home-assistant/1026746)
- iPad benchmark (iPad Pro 12.9" 2020, A12Z, Safari): textures capped at 512 px on touch (texture memory ~89 MB -> ~49 MB); ~1.5 MP pixel budget on touch; no MSAA; **render on demand**; `pagehide` releases the WebGL context; scene of ~623 objects, ~499,000 triangles, ~1,030 draw calls, ~2.5 s load on LAN — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/my-house-as-a-live-3d-dashboard-blender-model-three-js-driven-by-home-assistant/1026746)
- iOS-specific bug: a shader read `array[-1]` in a branch that never executes; Metal evaluated both branches and crashed the iPad ~5 s after load; fix was clamping dynamic array indices — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/my-house-as-a-live-3d-dashboard-blender-model-three-js-driven-by-home-assistant/1026746)
- A second builder in the same thread runs a comparable project as an HA sidebar panel that reuses the HA session, with dynamic camera views and YAML entity mappings — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/my-house-as-a-live-3d-dashboard-blender-model-three-js-driven-by-home-assistant/1026746)

#### E. Pre-rendered image composites (2.5D; the classic "Sweet Home 3D / Blender render + light overlays")
- The canonical technique: render one "night" base image per floor plus **one render per light with only that light on**, then stack them in a `picture-elements` card with CSS `mix-blend-mode: lighten`; a day image is swapped/faded in based on `sun.sun` (search summary of the long-running threads, started 2020 and still active at page 40) — [Floorplan UI with Color synced lights](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/floorplan-ui-with-color-synced-lights/169417); [3D Floorplan with Hue & Saturation mapped to RGB lights](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/3d-floorplan-with-hue-saturation-mapped-to-individual-blended-rgb-light-entities/168995)
- Best written-up variant (22 May 2024 — older, but the method is unchanged): SketchUp Make 2017 + Twilight Render ray-tracing; per floor: one night base, one sun-lit ambient image, a base+ambient pair per door/window state, and one image per light; composited and masked in GIMP ("Lighten only"), exported as aligned PNG layers — [Sean Blanchfield, "My smart home dashboard"](https://seanblanchfield-blog.pages.dev/2024/05/my-smart-home-dashboard)
- Runtime formulas from that write-up: overlay `opacity = brightness / 255`; colour via CSS `hue-rotate(hs_hue − 28°)` with saturation ×3 capped at 100 % (tuned by eye); ambient layer opacity `0.1 × (lux − 50)` clamped 0-100 % from an outdoor luminance sensor; door/window layers shown/hidden with `display` from contact sensors; image overlays have `tap_action: none` and separate `state-icon`/`state-label` elements on top take the clicks — [Sean Blanchfield](https://seanblanchfield-blog.pages.dev/2024/05/my-smart-home-dashboard)
- Limitations stated: every door/window state needs new base and ambient renders; multi-light rooms need each light's layer hand-masked to stop colour-shift bleeding; depends on card-mod — [Sean Blanchfield](https://seanblanchfield-blog.pages.dev/2024/05/my-smart-home-dashboard)
- Known colour problem in the forum variant: the overlay stays white in `color_temp` mode even when set warm (search summary) — [Floorplan UI with Color synced lights](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/floorplan-ui-with-color-synced-lights/169417)

#### F. SVG and 2D editors
- **ha-floorplan** (ExperienceLovelace; SVG whose elements are bound to entities via rules): latest release found is v1.1.4, which adds an experimental `card_set` service for embedding cards inside the SVG; still receiving releases, exact date not confirmed (search summary) — [newreleases.io v1.1.4](https://newreleases.io/project/github/ExperienceLovelace/ha-floorplan/release/v1.1.4); [project docs](https://experiencelovelace.github.io/ha-floorplan/about)
- **Houseplan** (first post 29 Jul 2026, latest update 14 Nov 2026): click-only editor, rooms bound to HA areas so devices place themselves; glowing light pools; sun shining through windows at the correct angle; **robot-vacuum live position with route trails**; walls with thickness; kiosk mode — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/houseplan-interactive-floor-plan/1019246)
- **Device Floorplan** (public beta, 2026): custom integration with a visual no-YAML 2D floorplan editor and live card (search summary) — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/device-floorplan-a-visual-floorplan-editor-and-live-dashboard-card-without-yaml-public-beta/1031497)
- **ha-home-map** (1 star, 13 commits, no releases — very small): canvas-drawn sidebar map auto-generated from HA areas/floors with temperature/humidity colour overlays, per-room presence indicators, door/window indicators, coloured light glow, and a PV/battery/grid summary — [GitHub](https://github.com/tomchambers2/ha-home-map)

### Inferences
- The community has converged on one architecture for live 3D: **glTF/GLB + three.js + HA WebSocket, entity IDs carried either in glTF extras or a side YAML/JSON map, render-on-demand, tap = toggle / long-press = detail**. A bespoke Svelte front end can adopt the same architecture directly; none of the cards needs to be embedded.
- Three distinct lighting strategies are in use, in ascending GPU cost: (1) emissive-only (3DHome), (2) fake light pools/glow meshes clipped to room polygons (Hollako; Floor3DX `light_rooms`), (3) real three.js lights with shadow maps, capped by a nearest-to-camera pool (bespoke build: 6 on touch, 14 on desktop) or by texture units (floor3d mod). Strategy 2 or the pooled form of 3 is what authors themselves recommend for phones and tablets.
- The fact that the most performance-focused fork ships a **"render once to a static tappable 2D image" companion** for tablets is strong evidence that always-on real-time 3D is still considered too heavy for cheap wall displays; a "bake the 3D view to an image, overlay live state in DOM/SVG" fallback tier is a design pattern worth copying for the low-power TV/wall display.
- Fit for the target environment: pre-rendered 2.5D composites (E) and SVG (F) are realistic everywhere including a low-power TV browser; three.js WebGL scenes of roughly 0.5 M triangles / ~1,000 draw calls are demonstrated on a 2020 iPad in Safari with texture caps and on-demand rendering; the same scene on a low-power TV browser is unproven and should be assumed to need the static fallback.
- Almost every 2026 project is a single maintainer and under six months old, several with multiple releases per week. They are good idea sources but poor dependencies.
- For a single-storey ~14-room house with yard, pool, driveway and gate, the bespoke build's approach (procedural Blender script -> one Draco glTF with extras) scales naturally to exterior features, whereas the "draw rooms in HA" editors are interior/room-centric (NeonPlan/NextFloor mention garden and parking; others do not).

### Gaps
- No evidence found of any Home Assistant card or integration that renders **Gaussian splats** or uses WebGPU specifically; searches returned only mesh-based (OBJ/GLB) projects.
- Rendering engine of Diorama, NeonPlan 3D, NextFloor, Homeslice and Zircon3D was not stated in the sources read (three.js is likely but unconfirmed).
- Last-commit dates and star counts could not be read for most repos; maintenance status above rests on forum post dates and version cadence.
- No official Home Assistant (Nabu Casa / Open Home Foundation) 3D or floor-plan feature was found in 2025-2026; I did not review the HA release notes directly, so absence is not confirmed.
- Reddit and YouTube showcase content was not reachable through search in this session; popular Blender day/night YouTube builds are therefore not individually documented here.
- The thread "3D Floorplan - Sweet Home 3d files natively wireframe rendered in Home Assistant" (2026) surfaced but was not read.

## 2. How commercial products do it (Samsung, LG, Aqara/Xiaomi, Apple, Matterport, others)

### Takeaway
Samsung SmartThings Map View and LG ThinQ 3D Home View are the only mainstream consumer products found that show a stylised 3D model of the user's actual floor plan with live device state; both are room-and-icon based (tap a device icon -> control card; filter the map by Energy/Temperature/Lighting) rather than photoreal, and both build the model from a floor-plan image, a database lookup, manual drawing or (Samsung) a robot-vacuum LiDAR map. Apple's October 2026 home hub shipped with no spatial/floor-plan view, and Aqara's "spatial intelligence" is about sensing, not visualisation.

### Cited Findings
- **Samsung SmartThings Map View** creation options in the app: take a picture of your floor plan, freehand draw with a finger or S Pen, or arrange rooms and walls from a preset layout; then a Layout tab (floors, walls, dividers), an Interior tab (windows, doors, stairs, furniture, decorations) and a Device tab — [Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10004030/)
- Device placement pattern: **drag rooms from the bottom of the screen onto the map and the devices in each room are added to that spot**; a "Device size" control adjusts icon size; "each room corresponds to the rooms in your SmartThings app" — [Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10004030/)
- Interaction pattern: double-tap to enlarge, tap a room to see its device total, **tap a device icon to open its control card**, a **2D/3D toggle** at the bottom, a Rotate button that steps the view, an "All devices" filter for **Energy, Temperature or Lighting**, and a usage bar opening hourly/daily/weekly/monthly energy breakdowns; settings can show routines and weather on the map or add a "pet" character that reports device status — [Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10004030/)
- 3D Map View launched globally on 9 April 2024 after CES 2024 (2D Map View rolled out in Korea and the US in 2023); it can use "Spatial AI" with **LiDAR in devices such as the Bespoke Jet Bot** to measure and map the home; shown on phones, Samsung TVs released after 2024 (DU7000 and above) and Family Hub fridges; "AI characters" were promised for end-2024 — [Samsung UK newsroom](https://news.samsung.com/uk/samsung-launches-3d-map-view-feature-based-on-smartthings-and-ai)
- Samsung also described address lookup that auto-creates a layout where the home's floor plan is available online (search summary) — [Samsung US newsroom](https://news.samsung.com/us/smartthings-revolutionizes-home-visualization-with-introduction-of-map-view)
- **LG ThinQ "3D Home View"** launched in the Korean ThinQ app in August 2024: appliances shown by room on a floor plan matching the home, with real-time product notifications and a switch from the plan to a 3D view of each room; initially only for apartments and officetels and not supported on phones with less than 6 GB RAM (search summary) — [LG Korea notice, Aug 2024](https://www.lge.co.kr/support/mobile-sw-update-SW20240827414003)
- LG's May 2025 update added a "draw floor plan" option when the plan is not found automatically, plus a tool to edit the layout structure (search summary) — [LG Korea notice, May 2025](https://www.lge.co.kr/support/software-SW20250521481001)
- **Apple**: the "HomeView" hub was announced 13 Oct 2026 (7-inch display, $299, launched 13 Nov 2026) with a "Home Modular" face for accessory access and a "Home Camera" face showing **up to nine camera feeds at once**; the interface adapts to viewer distance and recognises up to six faces. **No floor plan, room map or 3D/spatial view of the home is mentioned** — [MacRumors](https://macrumors.com/2026/10/13/apple-announces-homeview-smart-home-hub)
- **Aqara**: 2025-2026 "spatial intelligence" announcements concern sensing (e.g. Spatial Multi-Sensor FP400 covering up to 10 m × 8 m with a 120° field of view, and an app "Soft Sensor" inferring occupancy); no 3D floor-plan view in the Aqara Home app was found (search summary) — [Aqara CES 2026 press release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260106653294/en); [Aqara IFA 2025 press release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250904310919/en/Aqara-Showcases-Next-Gen-Smart-Homes-Powered-by-Spatial-Intelligence-at-IFA-2025)
- **Matterport + IoT**: partner SIMLAB's SIM-ON overlays IoT device information and controls on a Matterport twin; a Japanese homebuilder hands homeowners an iPad dashboard overlaid on the Matterport scan of their house; SIMLAB is working with KNX. Claims are vendor-reported (search summary) — [Matterport case study](https://matterport.com/industries/case-studies/simlab-integrates-bim-and-smart-building-iot-technologies-matterport)

### Inferences
- The commercial pattern is deliberately conservative: stylised extruded floor plan, device **icons** (not modelled fixtures) pinned to room positions, a 2D/3D toggle, stepped rotation instead of free orbit, and **layer filters** (energy / temperature / lighting) that recolour the same map. This is cheap to render and works on TVs and fridges; it is a realistic baseline tier for a low-power wall display.
- Room-bound auto-placement ("drag a room, its devices come with it") is shared by Samsung and by the 2026 HA editors (Homeslice, NeonPlan, Houseplan all bind rooms to HA areas). For a custom front end with HA areas already defined, auto-seeding device positions by area and letting the user nudge them is the established pattern.
- None of the commercial products found show live per-person position dots; presence is at most room-level.
- Community builds (Section 1D) already exceed the commercial products in fidelity (real sun, per-lamp lighting, door animation, PV flows), so commercial apps are more useful as interaction references than visual ones.

## 3. Capturing the house geometry cheaply

### Takeaway
There are three practical routes in 2026: (a) parametric — Apple RoomPlan on a LiDAR iPhone/iPad producing clean boxes-and-planes USDZ that must be converted to glTF (typically through Blender); (b) hand-modelled — Sweet Home 3D / Blender / SketchUp exported to GLB, which is what nearly every HA 3D project actually uses; (c) photoreal — phone Gaussian-splat capture (Scaniverse, Polycam, Luma, KIRI). For the web, the proven optimisation recipe is glTF-Transform with Meshopt or Draco geometry compression plus WebP/KTX2 textures capped at 1024-2048 px, which community projects report as 30 MB -> ~4 MB for a whole house.

### Cited Findings
- **RoomPlan**: `StructureBuilder` merges several captured rooms into one `CapturedStructure` that can be exported to a single USDZ (introduced at WWDC 2023) (search summary) — [Apple WWDC23 session 10192](https://developer.apple.com/wwdc23/10192)
- RoomPlan USD export options distinguish size-dependent primitives (parametric) from triangle mesh output; mesh is the default when no option is set (search summary) — [Apple docs: USDExportOptions](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/roomplan/capturedroom/usdexportoptions.md)
- RoomPlan export is one-way: there is no API to load a USDZ back into RoomPlan types; developers also report an `exceedSceneSizeLimit` error on repeated/large scans (search summary) — [Apple Developer Forums thread 831648](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/831648)
- USDZ in the browser: three.js has a `USDZLoader` but "its features are limited"; three.js contributors say USD was not designed for web delivery and recommend converting to glTF, with Blender's glTF exporter as the workflow most three.js developers use (search summary) — [three.js forum](https://discourse.threejs.org/t/usdz-loader-for-three-js/35357); the same question for RoomPlan output is asked on [Apple Developer Forums thread 764120](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/764120)
- **Hand modelling is the norm for HA 3D projects**: cards take models "made in Sweet Home 3D, Blender or SketchUp, exported as obj or glb", with GLB recommended over OBJ for speed and size — [Floor3D Pro thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/floor3d-pro-card-game-engine-backbone-edition/975682); [Hollako README](https://github.com/Hollako/Home-Assistant-3D-Floorplan)
- Authoring conventions that matter downstream: name meshes in the 3D editor so they can be referenced by animations/interactive objects (case-sensitive), and set object origins in Blender to the intended rotation centre (door hinges) — [Hollako README](https://github.com/Hollako/Home-Assistant-3D-Floorplan)
- A Sweet Home 3D exporter plugin exists that writes GLB/split glTF with an "optimize size and performance" option and HA-oriented light naming and extras metadata — [3DHome](https://github.com/lukevink/3DHome)
- Procedural alternative: generate the house from plans with a headless Blender Python script, treating plans and photos as ground truth; result ~499k triangles, 7.8 MB Draco glTF. The author notes hand-measured mounting heights/furniture and timber-frame elements 5-20 cm off in places — [live 3D dashboard thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/my-house-as-a-live-3d-dashboard-blender-model-three-js-driven-by-home-assistant/1026746)
- Typical sizes: "a small house model" was 30 MB before compression and about 4 MB after Draco/Meshopt + KTX2/WebP — [Floor3DX thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/floor3dx-card-the-3d-floor-plan-card-plus-two-optional-companions-a-2d-snapshot-card-and-a-learning-enabled-scheduler/1029922)
- **Optimisation recipe**: shrink textures first (cap at 1024 or 2048; WebP for download size, **KTX2 for GPU memory**), then quantise, then compress geometry with Meshopt or Draco, simplify only if still dense; starting command `gltf-transform optimize in.glb out.glb --compress meshopt --texture-compress webp --texture-size 1024`; run `gltf-transform inspect` first (search summary) — [GLB optimisation guide](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/optimize-glb-for-web)
- Meshopt vs Draco: sources disagree on size (one says Draco is 10-20 % smaller; another says only "a bit smaller" with a ~10× larger, slower decoder); the two cannot be mixed in one file; Draco needs a `DRACOLoader`; default texture settings can make PNGs larger, so pass an explicit codec (search summary) — [GLB optimisation guide](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/optimize-glb-for-web)
- Merging static meshes is as important as compression: 3,054 -> 401 draw calls, 26 ms -> 8 ms CPU frame time — [Floor3DX thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/floor3dx-card-the-3d-floor-plan-card-plus-two-optional-companions-a-2d-snapshot-card-and-a-learning-enabled-scheduler/1029922)
- Robot-vacuum maps and floor-plan images are used as tracing underlays to generate walls — [Homeslice thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/homeslice-your-home-as-a-live-3d-floor-plan-with-the-editor-built-into-home-assistant/1032185); floor-plan photo -> automatic 3D layout is how Samsung does it — [Samsung US support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10004030/)
- LiDAR helps mainly on textureless surfaces (white walls, dark areas, reflective floors); LiDAR devices are iPhone 12 Pro-16 Pro and iPad Pros since 2020 — [mobile capture guide, Mar 2026](https://polyvia3d.pages.dev/guides/gaussian-splatting-mobile-capture)
- Polycam's own format guide says GLB typically gives the smallest self-contained mesh file for web delivery (search summary) — [Polycam blog](https://poly.cam/blog/3d-model-file-formats-for-scanning-obj-fbx-glbgltf-usdz-stl-compared)

### Inferences
- For a single-storey 14-room house the cheapest accurate pipeline is likely: RoomPlan multi-room scan (or existing plans) as the dimensional reference -> rebuild or clean in Blender with named objects and correct hinge origins -> export GLB with extras -> `gltf-transform optimize` (Meshopt + WebP/KTX2). Raw RoomPlan parametric output is clean but untextured and interior-only, so yard, pool, driveway and gate must be modelled by hand or scripted anyway.
- A procedural Blender script (rebuild from a data file each run) is attractive for a long-lived personal project because re-exports are repeatable and entity metadata lives in one place; the bespoke build shows it is workable at whole-house scale.
- A target budget consistent with the published numbers is roughly 4-8 MB compressed glTF, about 0.5 M triangles or fewer, a few hundred draw calls after merging, and textures at or below 1024 px (512 px on touch devices).
- Raw photogrammetry/LiDAR meshes (Polycam/Scaniverse mesh mode) are a poor base for an interactive twin (no separable named objects, lumpy geometry); they are better used as a measuring reference or as a splat (Section 4).

### Gaps
- No measured RoomPlan USDZ file sizes for a whole house were found, and no end-to-end write-up of RoomPlan -> glTF -> HA dashboard.
- No reliable 2026 comparison of floor-plan-image-to-3D AI tools (accuracy, export formats) was found; only Samsung's in-app conversion is documented.
- Matterport export quality/sizes and Floorplanner glTF export were not researched.
- KTX2 behaviour on low-power TV browsers (transcoder cost, supported GPU formats) is unverified.

## 4. Gaussian splatting of home interiors/exteriors in 2025-2026

### Takeaway
Splats became a first-class web citizen in 2026: three.js r186 (September 2026) added a native `GaussianSplatMesh` with PLY/SPLAT/KSPLAT/SPZ loaders and a `KHR_gaussian_splatting` glTF loader extension, Spark 2.x offers a WebGL2 splat renderer for three.js with LoD and streaming, and the Khronos extension reached release candidate in February 2026 (one source reports ratification on 3 September 2026). A room is roughly 1-3 M Gaussians and 15-60 MB compressed. But a splat is baked lighting: toggling individual lamps realistically is research-grade (LuxRemix, CVPR 2026) and requires a special per-light capture/training pipeline, so in practice splats are a photoreal backdrop with hotspots and overlay effects, not a relightable twin.

### Cited Findings

#### Capture apps
- Scaniverse (Niantic): free, iOS and Android, exports SPZ natively. Polycam: freemium ($13/month full access), uses LiDAR depth where available, exports PLY and SPLAT. KIRI Engine: 5 free captures/month, PLY and SPLAT. Luma AI: iOS, PLY and SPLAT, built-in web viewer — [mobile capture guide, Mar 2026](https://polyvia3d.pages.dev/guides/gaussian-splatting-mobile-capture)
- Typical room-scale output: about 1-3 M Gaussians; test of a white kitchen gave 2.1 M Gaussians with no holes from LiDAR-assisted Polycam versus 1.8 M with three missing wall sections from photogrammetry-only Luma — [mobile capture guide](https://polyvia3d.pages.dev/guides/gaussian-splatting-mobile-capture)
- Interior capture guidance: diffuse light, avoid mixed light sources and direct sun; mirrors, glass and shiny metal cause artifacts; walk the perimeter then a pass through the middle; 100-300 frames for a 4×4 m room; practical limits about 50-200 m² indoors and 200-500 m² outdoors per capture — [mobile capture guide](https://polyvia3d.pages.dev/guides/gaussian-splatting-mobile-capture)

#### Formats and sizes
- Room-scale sizes (third-party estimates): PLY ~80-250 MB; `.splat` ~20-60 MB (4-6× smaller than PLY); Scaniverse SPZ ~15-25 MB; a 150 MB PLY converts to ~16-20 MB SPZ — [mobile capture guide](https://polyvia3d.pages.dev/guides/gaussian-splatting-mobile-capture)
- SPZ (open-sourced by Scaniverse/Niantic) claims about 90 % smaller than PLY at 64 bytes per Gaussian (search summary) — [Scaniverse SPZ announcement](https://scaniverse.com/news/spz-gaussian-splat-open-source-file-format)
- **SOG** (Spatially Ordered Gaussians, PlayCanvas, open source): lossy quantisation, "typically 15-20× smaller than an equivalent PLY" and about 2-3× smaller than Compressed PLY; converted with the `splat-transform` CLI (search summary) — [PlayCanvas SOG docs](https://developer.playcanvas.com/user-manual/gaussian-splatting/formats/sog/); [PlayCanvas blog](https://blog.playcanvas.com/playcanvas-open-sources-sog-format-for-gaussian-splatting/)
- Real-world counter-example: a user converted a 315 MB PLY to a 50 MB SOG at default settings (~6×) and found the result visibly degraded (search summary) — [PlayCanvas forum](https://forum.playcanvas.com/t/ply-vs-sog/42359)
- PlayCanvas describes SPZ as roughly 10× smaller than PLY and recommends SOG for web delivery (search summary) — [PlayCanvas SPZ docs](https://developer.playcanvas.com/user-manual/gaussian-splatting/formats/spz/)
- Per-splat storage by spherical-harmonic degree (bytes, Spark vs three.js): SH0 64 / 64; SH1 72 / 76; SH2 88 / 92; SH3 104 / 116 — [Ben Houston, 7 Aug 2026](https://ben3d.ca/blog/gaussian-splatting-for-threejs)

#### Khronos glTF extension status
- Khronos announced a **release candidate** of the baseline `KHR_gaussian_splatting` extension in February 2026, open for industry feedback before ratification — [AEC Magazine](https://aecmag.com/news/khronos-announces-gltf-gaussian-splatting-extension/); [Digital Production, 4 Feb 2026](https://digitalproduction.com/2026/02/04/gltf-learns-to-splat-without-losing-the-plot/)
- One secondary source reports the extension reached **"Complete, Ratified" on 3 September 2026** via a commit to the glTF extensions registry with no Khronos press release — [radiancefields.com Khronos page](https://radiancefields.com/platforms/the-khronos-group). Conflict/uncertainty: no primary Khronos confirmation was found; other coverage only shows the release-candidate stage.
- What the base extension stores: position, rotation, scale, opacity and spherical-harmonic coefficients, with required `kernel` and `colorSpace` properties; **compression is not in the base spec** — it lives in nested extensions, the furthest along being the draft `KHR_gaussian_splatting_compression_spz_2`; a renderer that does not know the extension falls back to drawing a sparse point cloud via `COLOR_0`; known implementations include CesiumJS, Cesium Native and JSplat, with Babylon.js and PlayCanvas also shipping support — [radiancefields.com Khronos page](https://radiancefields.com/platforms/the-khronos-group)

#### Web renderers
- **three.js r186** (reported shipped 8 Sep 2026 as npm `three@0.186.0`) supports splats natively: `GaussianSplatMesh` (WebGPU/TSL NodeMaterial), loaders `SPZLoader`, `SPLATLoader`, `KSPLATLoader`, PLY via `PLYLoader` + helper, and glTF `KHR_gaussian_splatting` through `GLTFGaussianSplatLoaderExtension` (registered manually); SOG is not in the list (search summary + article) — [radiancefields.com three.js page](https://radiancefields.com/platforms/three-js); [radiancefields.com r186 article](https://radiancefields.com/three.js-merges-a-native-gaussian-splat-renderer-for-webgpu-in-r186)
- Native three.js renderer details: GPU counting sort with 4,096 depth bins in four TSL compute passes, re-sorting only when the camera moves past a threshold; it also runs on the WebGL backend of `WebGPURenderer` using a **CPU sort fallback**; ~10 KB gzip; ray casting treats each splat as an ellipsoid. Not included: level of detail, streaming, progressive loading, WASM, workers; scene-scale (multi-room) streaming is "future work" — [Ben Houston](https://ben3d.ca/blog/gaussian-splatting-for-threejs); [radiancefields.com r186 article](https://radiancefields.com/three.js-merges-a-native-gaussian-splat-renderer-for-webgpu-in-r186)
- Conflict: the merge article says colour is SH0 only (no SH1-SH3), while the author's blog describes view-dependent colour up to SH3 computed in a WebGPU compute pre-pass — [radiancefields.com r186 article](https://radiancefields.com/three.js-merges-a-native-gaussian-splat-renderer-for-webgpu-in-r186); contradicted by [Ben Houston](https://ben3d.ca/blog/gaussian-splatting-for-threejs)
- **Spark** (`@sparkjsdev/spark`, World Labs): "built for THREE.js and WebGL2", targets 98 %+ WebGL2 devices including low-powered mobile; `SplatMesh` extends `THREE.Object3D`; splats render together with triangle meshes in the normal `render(scene, camera)` call and multiple splat objects sort correctly together; real-time colour editing, displacement and a GPU shader-graph system ("Dyno"); the docs say new recolouring and relighting techniques are "expected in the future" — [Spark docs overview](https://sparkjs.dev/docs/overview/)
- Spark reads PLY, SPZ, SPLAT, KSPLAT and SOG and needs three.js r180 or later (search summary) — [Spark GitHub](https://github.com/sparkjsdev/spark); Spark 2.x adds LoD splat trees, virtual paging and the `.RAD` format with 64K-splat chunks fetched by HTTP range requests — [Ben Houston](https://ben3d.ca/blog/gaussian-splatting-for-threejs); v2.3.0 fixed on-demand rendering for paged LoD (search summary) — [radiancefields.com](https://radiancefields.com/spark-v2.3.0-fixes-on-demand-rendering-for-paged-lod)
- An experimental WebGPU renderer for Spark ("Radix") exists but 11 of 42 examples are blocked by LoD selection, paged RAD streaming, SOG or skinning (search summary) — [radiancefields.com](https://radiancefields.com/spark-radix-brings-webgpu-splat-rendering-to-threejs)

#### Hotspots and interactivity
- PlayCanvas SuperSplat Studio supports **annotations: 3D-positioned text hotspots, each pairing a title/description with a saved camera view**, with a navigation bar to step through them (search summary) — [SuperSplat Studio docs](https://developer.playcanvas.com/user-manual/supersplat/studio/); [PlayCanvas blog](https://blog.playcanvas.com/build-gaussian-splat-experiences-with-supersplat-studio)
- People building tours report that naive image/plane hotspots pop and sort unreliably against the splat (search summary) — [PlayCanvas forum](https://forum.playcanvas.com/t/hotspots-around-a-gaussian-splat/41325)

#### Relighting / toggling lights
- **LuxRemix** (CVPR 2026) decomposes an indoor scene's lighting into individual sources that can each be toggled, recoloured or dimmed, by training a 3DGS model that stores **independent per-light HDR coefficients per Gaussian**; demonstrated in real time in a modified Nerfstudio viewer, not a web viewer (search summary) — [arXiv 2601.15283](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15283)
- A browser demo of "Relightable Gaussian Splats" exists (lighting toggle), scope not verified (search summary) — [andrewkchan.dev/lit-splat](https://andrewkchan.dev/lit-splat)
- Offline tools are adding splat relighting (Nuke applies scene lights and shadows to splats; V-Ray 7 Update 3) — these are DCC/compositing workflows, not web runtime (search summary) — [Foundry Nuke docs](https://learn.foundry.com/nuke/content/comp_environment/splats/relighting_splats.html); [radiancefields.com on V-Ray](https://radiancefields.com/v-ray-7-update-3-adds-gaussian-splat-relighting)

#### Browser reality on iOS
- Safari 26 enables WebGPU by default on iOS/iPadOS 26, and WebGPU is now in all major browsers (search summary) — [web.dev](https://web.dev/blog/webgpu-supported-major-browsers)
- WebKit bug reports describe the GPU process being killed for memory on iPadOS 26 and time-dependent WebGPU crashes on iPhone 15 Pro / 16 Pro Max under iOS 26.0-26.1; one engine vendor reported disabling WebGPU on iOS and falling back to WebGL; a WebKit maintainer notes iOS terminates under memory pressure far earlier than macOS (search summary; individual bugs not read) — [WebKit bug 303203](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=303203); [WebKit bug 301627](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=301627); [WebKit bug 302711](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=302711)
- An older (2023) WebKit report of three.js pages crashing right after low-memory warnings reportedly still reproduced in the Safari 26 beta (search summary) — [WebKit bug 261685](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=261685)

### Inferences
- **Realistic in the target environment:** a single room or the exterior/yard as a 1-3 M-Gaussian SPZ/SOG (15-50 MB) rendered with Spark on WebGL2 is plausible on recent iPhones/iPads and desktop; a whole 14-room interior as one splat (tens of millions of Gaussians) is not, without Spark 2.x LoD/paged streaming — and even then it is unproven on iOS Safari PWAs given memory-pressure kills. On a low-power TV/wall-display browser, splats should be assumed infeasible; use a pre-rendered image or video of the splat instead.
- **WebGPU-only paths are risky on iOS in late 2026**; the WebGL2 path (Spark, or three.js native on the WebGL backend with CPU sorting) is the safer choice for an installed PWA.
- Because both Spark and the three.js native renderer place splats in an ordinary three.js scene, the workable hybrid is: **splat as photoreal backdrop + invisible or low-poly proxy meshes for picking + three.js/DOM overlays for live state** (emissive halos, sprites, lines). Hotspots should be depth-tested meshes/sprites or screen-space DOM anchors rather than transparent planes, which sources say sort badly against splats.
- "Relight when a lamp turns on" options, in order of practicality: (1) capture two or more splats (lights off / lights on, or day / night) and cross-fade them; (2) use Spark's per-splat colour editing/Dyno to tint or brighten Gaussians within a radius of the lamp position (an approximation, not physically correct — Spark itself lists relighting as future work); (3) LuxRemix-style per-light decomposition, which needs a custom training pipeline and a custom shader port. No evidence was found of anyone doing (1) or (2) for a smart-home dashboard, so these are design proposals, not documented practice.
- The exterior (yard, pool, driveway, gate) is the best splat candidate for this house: it is hard to hand-model convincingly, its lighting is dominated by the sun (a day and a night capture cover most states), and interaction is limited to a few hotspots (gate, pool pump, cameras, beams).

### Gaps
- No measured frame rates or maximum splat counts for iPhone/iPad Safari PWAs were found for any renderer.
- No primary Khronos source confirming ratification of `KHR_gaussian_splatting` was located.
- No example was found of a splat driven by live smart-home state (Home Assistant or otherwise).
- Whole-house splat sizes (as opposed to single-room) and Spark `.RAD` real-world numbers were not found.
- Whether splat picking via Spark's raycasting is accurate enough for small targets (a lamp, a switch) was not covered in the pages read.

## 5. Live presence and position inside the model

### Takeaway
Only 24 GHz mmWave radars built on the HLK-LD2450 (Everything Presence Lite/Pro, Apollo R-PRO-1, Screek, DIY ESPHome) give Home Assistant true per-person x/y coordinates today — up to three targets, about 6 m range and a ±60° fan, in millimetres relative to the sensor, with jitter of hundreds of millimetres and unstable target IDs. BLE (Bermuda, ESPresense) is room-level in practice, with experimental trilateration reporting about 1 m at best and visible room-flapping; Aqara FP2 exposes zones only; Wi-Fi CSI sensing is zone-level; no shipping UWB solution was found. The first HA 3D card to draw live radar targets as people in the model appeared in September 2026.

### Cited Findings

#### mmWave with x/y coordinates
- HLK-LD2450: 24 GHz FMCW radar, max range around 6 m, azimuth roughly ±60° (fan-shaped coverage), up to three simultaneous targets; ESPHome exposes X, Y, speed, angle, distance and direction per target. Coordinates are millimetres with the sensor as origin: X −3000 to +3000 (negative = left), Y 0 to 6000 forward — [esp32.co.uk, 2 Sep 2026](https://esp32.co.uk/ld2450-zones-home-assistant-coordinates-exclusion-zones/)
- Noise and latency indicators: live coordinates "can wander by hundreds of millimetres"; ESPHome by default throttles numeric target sensors to about one second and applies a one-second settle filter to binary sensors — [esp32.co.uk](https://esp32.co.uk/ld2450-zones-home-assistant-coordinates-exclusion-zones/)
- Known failure modes: a person can swap between Target 1 and Target 2 (build logic on zone counts, not target numbers); seated/reclining people drift out of tight zones or vanish (pair with an LD2410 for stillness); fans, curtains, doors and activity through thin partitions create false tracks; remounting or rotating the sensor invalidates the whole coordinate map — [esp32.co.uk](https://esp32.co.uk/ld2450-zones-home-assistant-coordinates-exclusion-zones/)
- Zone calibration practice: mount rigidly, mark the centre line as X = 0, **walk the edges of each area while watching live X/Y and record ranges**, start with generous rectangles and tighten only where false occupancy appears; "Filter" mode turns rectangles into exclusion zones (one mode for the whole radar) — [esp32.co.uk](https://esp32.co.uk/ld2450-zones-home-assistant-coordinates-exclusion-zones/)
- The usual "dots on a plot" view is a **Plotly Graph card** fed by the Target 1-3 X/Y sensors, on which zones are drawn around observed tracks (search summary) — [Apollo Automation wiki](https://wiki.apolloautomation.com/products/rpro1/setup/zones-ha/)
- Everything Presence Lite/Pro (both LD2450-based): a **Zone Configurator** add-on with a visual interface for drawing zones and watching real-time target tracking, coloured dots per person, and **trail recording** to debug zone placement; up to 4 detection zones and 2 exclusion zones; target entities update frequently and can bloat the recorder database (search summary) — [Everything Smart docs](https://docs.everythingsmart.io/s/products/doc/zone-configurator-93b9scGsa2)
- **Everything Presence Pro Grid** (community firmware/integration, 2026): a perspective-corrected, calibrated top-down grid of **30 cm × 30 cm cells** with painted named zones, furniture overlays and Entry/Exit, Interference and Suppress overlays; live dots in a fixed colour per target slot with a 1-9 signal label; stuck targets auto-dismissed after 5 minutes; combines LD2450 with a SEN0609 static-presence radar and PIR — [live overview docs](https://clintongormley.github.io/everything-presence-pro-grid/user-guide/live-overview/); [community thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/everything-presence-pro-grid-a-perspective-corrected-grid-based-zone-engine-for-the-epp-sensor/1010187)
- **NAMO** (ESP32 + LD2450; first post 14 Jul 2026, v0.5.5 beta 19 Jul, fusion update 27 Aug 2026): upload a floor-plan image, enter its physical scale, rooms are auto-detected; a guided flow places and rotates the radar on the plan; detection, exclusion, correction and exit zones; **up to seven devices form an overlap group sharing floor-plan coordinates, merging observations of the same person** using coordinates, movement, uncertainty and track identity; a last reliable position stays on the map in grey while an LD2410C still holds presence. Author's caveats: motionless people remain the main challenge and unstable Wi-Fi can create temporary duplicate targets — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/namo-on-device-floorplan-tracking-and-visual-zones-with-esp32-and-ld2450/1017317)
- **In a 3D model**: floor3d-card mod (Sep 2026) supports person trackers fed by coordinate-reporting radars such as the HLK-LD2450; trackers are recalculated only when their entities change and the marker disappears when the person leaves; positions are `[x, y, z]` — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/floor3d-card-mod-updated-fork-of-the-3d-floor-plan-card-three-js-0-186-lit-3-sunlight-new-editor/1026920); [FORK.md](https://github.com/giosci1994/floor3d-card/blob/master/FORK.md)
- **Aqara FP2**: integrates locally via HomeKit Controller and is exposed as multiple zone-presence sensors; zones are drawn in the Aqara app and synced; sources found show no target-coordinate entity in Home Assistant (search summary) — [Derek Seaman, 2023](https://www.derekseaman.com/2023/04/home-assistant-setting-up-the-aqara-fp2-presence-sensor.html); [jpk.io 2026 guide](https://jpk.io/home-automation/aqara-fp2-zones-home-assistant/)
- Aqara's FP400 "Spatial Multi-Sensor" (CES 2026) covers up to 10 m × 8 m with a 120° field of view (search summary) — [Aqara press release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260106653294/en)

#### BLE
- **Bermuda**: room-level tracking by nearest proxy (ESPHome Bluetooth proxies assigned to HA areas) plus per-proxy distance estimates; its own docs say "A 2D map of your house is not yet a thing. A 3D map is even less of a thing" (search summary) — [Bermuda wiki](https://github.com/agittins/bermuda/wiki); [Bermuda repo](https://github.com/agittins/bermuda)
- Bermuda's author on RSSI: "Rssi is just not capable of accuracy, for most definitions of accurate" (search summary) — [Bermuda community thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/bermuda-bluetooth-ble-room-presence-and-tracking-custom-integration/625780)
- Field data point: in the live-3D-dashboard thread, Bermuda switched a **stationary** beacon between rooms 33 times in 48 hours; another builder then announced his own Bluetooth trilateration service "accurate to about 1 m" — [thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/my-house-as-a-live-3d-dashboard-blender-model-three-js-driven-by-home-assistant/1026746)
- **ble-trilateration** (Bermuda fork, 7 stars, no releases): weighted least-squares 3D solve across anchors with x/y/z in metres, calibration fingerprints recorded for 60 s per known position, floor reachability gating via stairs/lifts, room hysteresis. It outputs Area and Distance sensors only — x/y/z appear only in a diagnostic dump the author says is not a stable API; no accuracy figures — [GitHub](https://github.com/philbert/ble-trilateration)
- **ESPresense Companion**: YAML floors with bounds in metres, rooms as `[x, y]` polygons from a bottom-left origin, nodes at absolute `[x, y, z]`; locators `nadaraya_watson`, `nelder_mead`, `bfgs`, `mle`, `multi_floor`, `nearest_node`; Kalman filter plus exponential smoothing (`max_velocity` 0.5 m/s, `smoothing_weight` 0.7); device stale after 30 s, away after 120 s; guidance is nodes at the floor-plan corners and at least 5 fixes. **No numeric accuracy figure is published** — [ESPresense Companion configuration](https://espresense.com/companion/configuration/)

#### Wi-Fi sensing and UWB
- Wi-Fi CSI sensing (TOMMY): turns ESP32-C5/C6 devices into presence sensors exposing each zone as a motion `binary_sensor` over MQTT or Matter; the vendor admits zones may detect presence outside the intended zone — zone-level, not coordinates (search summary) — [tommysense.com](https://www.tommysense.com/)
- UWB: only forum ideas were found (e.g. phone-pointing control via UWB anchors; the author notes Google has not opened UWB APIs on Android) — no shipping HA integration (search summary) — [community thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/half-baked-idea-phone-pointing-control-for-ha-via-uwb/1019012)

#### Robot vacuums as "position dots"
- Vacuum live position with route trails is drawn on 2D plans (Houseplan) and in 3D; one builder notes a eufy position updates only every 40-90 s, so the robot glides up to 8 m between points, and that a PHP proxy was needed for the Roborock map image because HA sends no CORS header — [Houseplan thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/houseplan-interactive-floor-plan/1019246); [live 3D dashboard thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/my-house-as-a-live-3d-dashboard-blender-model-three-js-driven-by-home-assistant/1026746)

### Inferences
- Showing radar targets in a house model is a 2D rigid transform per sensor: each radar needs a position (x, y), a mounting rotation and a mirror flag in house coordinates; target (X mm, Y mm) -> rotate -> translate -> metres. Calibration can copy NAMO's approach (place/rotate the radar on the plan, then refine from captured target positions) and the "walk the edges" method.
- Because target IDs swap and coordinates jitter by hundreds of millimetres at roughly 1 Hz through ESPHome defaults, the front end should render **anonymous presence blobs with smoothing/easing and a fade-out**, not identified avatars; a persistent grey "last known" marker (NAMO) handles stationary people. Identity ("who") can only come from BLE at room level, so a sensible fusion is: radar gives where, BLE room gives who, attach a name only when exactly one person and one radar target share a room.
- With only three targets per sensor, 6 m range and a 120° fan, a 14-room house would need roughly one radar per room of interest; overlapping sensors need de-duplication (NAMO merges by coordinates, movement and uncertainty).
- BLE trilateration to about 1 m on a single storey is claimed by one hobbyist but not benchmarked anywhere found; treat BLE position dots as decorative/approximate and show them at room level (e.g. a person chip inside the room) rather than as precise coordinates.
- Subscribing to raw target entities through the HA WebSocket is fine for the browser, but those entities should be excluded from the recorder (sources warn about database growth).
- All of this is rendering-light (a few sprites or SVG circles) and realistic on every target device, including the low-power wall display.

### Gaps
- No published accuracy or latency measurements (in cm / ms) for LD2450 tracking were found; the sensor's native frame rate was not confirmed in the sources read.
- Aqara FP2/FP300 coordinate access from Home Assistant: no evidence found either way beyond zone entities; FP300 was not researched in detail.
- ESPresense Companion's 3D map view is not described in the documentation page read; no screenshots/behaviour could be documented.
- The Bluetooth trilateration write-up claiming about 1 m accuracy was referenced in a forum thread but not located or read.
- No Seeed-specific (e.g. MR60/MR24 series) x/y tracking material was reviewed.
- No outdoor person-position technique (yard/driveway) other than cameras was found; mmWave sources are all indoor.

## 6. Rendering live state on a spatial model

### Takeaway
In practice nobody blends true per-light baked lightmaps at runtime in the HA world: the two working approaches are (a) per-light pre-rendered images composited with `mix-blend-mode: lighten` and opacity = brightness (2.5D, runs anywhere), and (b) real-time three.js with either a capped pool of real lights or cheap fake light pools clipped to room polygons. Around that core, 2026 projects have converged on the same set of live overlays — animated doors/covers from hinge metadata, sun position and shadows, per-room heatmaps for temperature/humidity/CO2, animated PV/battery/grid flows, media art on TV screens, camera streams via go2rtc, and robot-vacuum trails.

### Cited Findings

#### Lighting
- 2.5D per-light compositing: one render per light, `mix-blend-mode: lighten`, `opacity = brightness/255`, colour by `hue-rotate()` with a hand-tuned offset and saturation boost; an ambient daylight layer faded by a lux sensor — [Sean Blanchfield, 2024](https://seanblanchfield-blog.pages.dev/2024/05/my-smart-home-dashboard)
- Emissive-only toggling: light state shown purely with emissive materials on named fixture meshes (no scene lights) — [3DHome](https://github.com/lukevink/3DHome)
- Fake light pools: per-light floor pools, wall/ceiling glow and an optional GI-bounce approximation, all clipped to room polygons, with Spot/Cove/Linear/Lamp presets and per-zone day/night opacity — [Hollako README](https://github.com/Hollako/Home-Assistant-3D-Floorplan)
- Real lights with a budget: fixed pool of point lights reassigned to the lamps nearest the camera (14 desktop / 6 touch) so shaders never recompile — [live 3D dashboard thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/my-house-as-a-live-3d-dashboard-blender-model-three-js-driven-by-home-assistant/1026746); shadow-casting lights limited by texture units (14 on a 16-unit GPU) with a low preset of sun + 4 lamps — [floor3d mod FORK.md](https://github.com/giosci1994/floor3d-card/blob/master/FORK.md); users report lag beyond 6 lights in an unpooled card — [Floor3D Pro thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/floor3d-pro-card-game-engine-backbone-edition/975682)
- Lights driven by entity brightness **and colour**, with physical light units, sRGB colour management and Neutral tone mapping, are now standard in the r186 forks — [floor3d mod thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/floor3d-card-mod-updated-fork-of-the-3d-floor-plan-card-three-js-0-186-lit-3-sunlight-new-editor/1026920); [Floor3DX thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/floor3dx-card-the-3d-floor-plan-card-plus-two-optional-companions-a-2d-snapshot-card-and-a-learning-enabled-scheduler/1029922)
- Baked lightmaps per light: no three.js/HA example was found. The pattern exists in game-engine discussion — one default lightmap for static lights plus a separate lightmap per switchable light composited on the GPU (search summary) — [Unity forum](https://discussions.unity.com/t/dynamic-on-and-off-baked-mixed-lights/923179); Unity documents the limitation that changing a baked light's colour at runtime leaves its baked indirect light unchanged (search summary) — [Unity manual](https://docs.unity3d.com/Manual/lighting-mode-runtime.html); `@react-three/lightmap` lists a composited multi-layer lightmap per light group only as a wishlist item (search summary) — [pmndrs/react-three-lightmap](https://github.com/pmndrs/react-three-lightmap)

#### Sun, sky, weather
- Sun position from real location/orientation with a time slider, cloud shadows, and night windows that glow from HA light state — [live 3D dashboard thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/my-house-as-a-live-3d-dashboard-blender-model-three-js-driven-by-home-assistant/1026746)
- Sunlight following `sun.sun` with time-of-day shadows; sky that greys with clouds; a moon — [floor3d mod thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/floor3d-card-mod-updated-fork-of-the-3d-floor-plan-card-three-js-0-186-lit-3-sunlight-new-editor/1026920); [FORK.md](https://github.com/giosci1994/floor3d-card/blob/master/FORK.md)

#### Doors, windows, covers, gates

#### Performance practices that recur
- Render on demand / redraw only on state change; stop the loop when idle or hidden — [live 3D dashboard thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/my-house-as-a-live-3d-dashboard-blender-model-three-js-driven-by-home-assistant/1026746); [Homeslice thread](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/homeslice-your-home-as-a-live-3d-floor-plan-with-the-editor-built-into-home-assistant/1032185); [3DHome](https://github.com/lukevink/3DHome)

### Inferences
- **Technique-by-environment fit (assessment from the cited evidence):**

| Technique | iPhone/iPad Safari PWA | Desktop Chrome/Edge | Low-power TV / wall display |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-rendered per-light image layers with CSS blend modes (2.5D) | Yes | Yes | Yes — the safest option |
| SVG / canvas floor plan with glow pools, dots, heat tints | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| three.js GLB, emissive toggles + fake clipped light pools, render on demand | Yes (demonstrated on tablets) | Yes | Probably, with a low-quality tier; unproven |
| three.js with real lights + shadow maps (pooled 4-6 lights) | Yes on recent devices with texture/pixel caps (demonstrated on a 2020 iPad Pro) | Yes (14+ lights) | Unlikely; use a static snapshot fallback |
| Per-light baked lightmaps blended in a custom shader | Plausible (texture memory is the constraint) but no precedent found | Plausible, no precedent found | Unknown |
| Gaussian splat backdrop (single room / exterior, WebGL2) | Plausible on recent devices; memory-kill risk | Yes | No |
| WebGPU-only rendering paths | Risky in iOS 26.0-26.1 per WebKit bug reports | Yes | No |
| Live video textures from go2rtc in the scene | Yes via HLS on Safari; keep the number of concurrent streams small | Yes via MSE | Doubtful beyond one stream |

- Per-light baked lightmaps would give the highest visual quality per GPU cost (full global illumination, no runtime lights), and the math is the same additive/lighten blend the 2.5D community already uses (sum of lightmap_i × brightness_i × colour_i). With roughly 40-60 lights it would need lightmap atlasing or grouping by room to fit texture memory on iOS. This is a design proposal extrapolated from the 2.5D practice and game-engine discussions — no web implementation was found.
- A hybrid that matches what the best community builds effectively do: baked ambient occlusion plus a day and a night ambient lightmap in the glTF, real-time sun as the only shadow caster, lamps as fake clipped pools or a small pool of real point lights near the camera.
- For this specific house: animated PV -> battery -> house -> grid flows, a sliding gate driven by a cover position, pool pump/temperature as an overlay on the pool, and robot/vacuum-style trails all have direct precedents above. **Perimeter security beams drawn as lines and camera frustums/fields of view have no precedent in the sources found**; they are straightforward three.js additions (line segments between beam posts coloured by binary_sensor state; translucent frustum meshes from camera position, heading and FOV) and would be novel relative to the community projects surveyed.
- Storing entity bindings and animation parameters (entity_id, hinge axis, open angle, room) in glTF extras at authoring time, as the bespoke build does, keeps the front end generic: the Svelte app can walk the scene graph once, build an entity -> object map, and apply WebSocket state changes without per-entity code.
- Tap = toggle, long-press = detail card (dimmer/colour), swipe = dim, and saved camera views per room are the interaction conventions shared by the bespoke build, NeonPlan/NextFloor and the floor3d forks; adopting them will match what users of these projects already expect.

### Gaps
- No implementation was found of volumetric (true 3D) temperature or CO2 "clouds"; all heatmaps found are floor/room-level tints, and the interpolation method (per-room versus inverse-distance between sensors) is undocumented in the sources read.
- No example was found of camera frustums, camera feeds pinned at the camera's 3D location, or security beams rendered as lines in a home model.
- No example was found of energy "flowing through walls" along real cable routes in a consumer project other than Zircon3D's unspecified "electrical routing" and the bespoke build's pipe/duct layers.
- No per-light baked-lightmap web example was found, so its memory cost on iOS (number of lightmaps × resolution) is untested.
- Performance of any of these techniques on a specific low-power TV browser (Tizen, webOS, Android TV WebView, Fire tablets beyond anecdote) was not measured in any source.
